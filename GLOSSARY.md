# MKM Quotations

MKM staff prepare travel quotations for clients comparing packages.

## Language

**Inquiry**

A group of alternative quotations for one client's booking decision. Each separate booking gets its own Inquiry, and a client can have at most one open Inquiry at a time.

**Closed Inquiry**

An Inquiry whose comparison has ended through quotation acceptance, staff closure without a selection, or automatic closure due to inactivity.

**Quotation Revision**

A saved version of a quotation's price and terms. All revisions of the same quotation represent one option in an Inquiry.

**Closed Quotation**

A quotation removed from consideration and kept as read-only history in its Inquiry.
